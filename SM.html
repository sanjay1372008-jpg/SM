A few helpful answers

Frequently considered.

What does bespoke perfume mean?

Bespoke perfume refers to a fragrance created or developed around an individual's preferences. A real service may include a consultation, exploration of notes, evaluation and refinement.

How should I test a new fragrance?

Begin with a small amount on skin, follow the product's directions and allow the scent to develop over several hours. Test one or two fragrances at a time so you can compare them comfortably.

What are top, heart and base notes?

Top notes provide the opening impression, heart notes establish the central character, and base notes contribute depth to the later stages of the fragrance.

How can I make my perfume last longer?

Longevity depends on the formula, application, skin and environment. Store perfume away from heat and sunlight. Apply as directed and avoid spraying into eyes or onto irritated skin.

Can I find a fragrance as a gift?

Consider the recipient's preferred notes, existing fragrances and typical occasions. A discovery set or gift card from a real, verified retailer can be a thoughtful option.

Are the prices on this website real?

No. The prices, product names and Maison Élan business identity on this concept website are illustrative. This website does not process real purchases.

Can I book a fragrance consultation here?

The form is a frontend demonstration. It can preview your fragrance brief locally, but it cannot reserve an appointment or contact an atelier.

Does the fragrance finder store my answers?

No. Your selections are processed in your browser to show a suggested fragrance direction. They are not submitted to a backend.
